# Disassembly of Progressive Cavity Pump

**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Y2UDLAOgRI

## Overview

This work instruction provides step-by-step guidelines for disassembling a Progressive Cavity Pump.

## Steps

### Step 1. Remove the set plug for the drive pin

Loosen the bolt and remove the set plug from the housing to access the drive pin.

### Step 2. Remove the hold down plug and drive pin

Screw out the hold down plug, go to the other side, remove the alignment tool, and lightly tap on the drive pin.

### Step 3. Remove the cap screws

Use a 3/8-inch drive ratchet with a 3/4-inch socket to remove the cap screws and the cap.

### Step 4. Mark the position of the cap

Place a mark on either side of the cap to ensure it is installed in the same position when reassembled.

### Step 5. Remove the stator and rotor

Use a large pipe wrench to loosen the stator and rotor from the housing. Use a leverage bar pipe to unscrew the stator and rotor.

### Step 6. Slide out the stator and rotor

Use a helper to slide the stator and rotor out of the housing assembly and set it on the ground.

### Step 7. Check the seal on the connecting rod

Verify that the seal on the end of the connecting rod is in place and not damaged.

### Step 8. Inspect the connecting rod washer

Check if the connecting rod washer is defective or not in place, which can cause corrosion and water seepage.
